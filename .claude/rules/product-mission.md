# What this product is for

The mission is written down once, in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) under "Why
this exists — the mission". Read it before proposing features or making a
design trade-off. The short version:

Friendly Learning exists to **connect the students of a university** — with
each other and with the people and activity around them. Faculty and student
discovery, events that show who is going (so strangers can go together), posts
as the live picture of campus, asking anything without shame (including saying
you are not doing well), every club and its activity in one place, and letting
any student start a community and lead it.

When two options compete, prefer the one that leads to a real connection
between people over one that only displays information.

If you change the mission text, change it in AGENTS.md and keep this summary in
step.
